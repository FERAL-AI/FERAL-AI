## Required product contracts

Fields described as proposed are requirements, not claims of implemented endpoints. Reuse existing modules only after verifying their behavior against these contracts.

### Identity, devices and task handoff

Derive `owner_id` from trusted authentication/enrollment, never an arbitrary body field. Separate owner, person, agent, conversation, turn, task, device and peer identities. Another session is not another privacy boundary. Same-owner continuity and cross-user sharing have different consent and authorization.

Bind a durable task to proposed `task_id`, owner, originating turn/source references, plan revision, action/review IDs, execution lease/generation, selected device, capability version and outcome evidence. Distinguish draft, awaiting review, authorized, running, awaiting external outcome, succeeded, failed, cancelled and outcome unknown. Approval, queue acceptance, ACK, remote report and verified completion are separate states. Reconnect must not replay an irreversible action; reconcile authoritative outcomes and require fresh review if scope changed.

Existing `api/routes/handoff.py` and `agents/session_handoff.py` transfer working context to a node class. Class-based pending handoff is not an exact owner-scoped device/task checkpoint. Add selected-device acceptance, expiry, deduplication and durable cursor/checkpoint semantics. Test two matching devices, offline targets, stale manifests, account switch, partial transfer and duplicate reconnect. A queued success receipt is not task completion.

### Memory and knowledge

Separate conversation events, confirmed/inferred facts, tasks, health observations and action/audit records. Retain sources, observation/ingestion times, owner/scope, quality/confidence and revision. Corrections outrank older inference; summaries cannot fabricate completion or replace authoritative records. An event ACK means durable acceptance, not completion of derivatives.

`memory/store.py`, wiki ingestion and scoped federation are foundations, not a comprehensive consumer owner partition or backup/deletion system. Build same-owner deduplication, revision conflict and tombstone handling. Demonstrate deletion of originals, summaries, vectors, uploads and replicas with truthful backup retention. Federation export/import is not full backup. Verify embedding-space changes and rollback before changing stored-vector models.

Imports bind reviewed immutable bytes or an exact bounded file manifest, reject unsafe traversal, disclose provider embedding transmission/partial writes and verify receipts. Do not use private folders in tests. Show stale derivatives honestly. Current wiki APIs do not justify invented edit/delete/full-erasure controls.

### Voice, camera and glasses

One coordinator owns microphone/playback. Correlate owner/device/session/turn/stream/request across partial/final text, audio and interruptions. Barge-in affects the exact output owner; late audio must not play after a new turn. Indicators, consent, stop and retention must match actual capture. Offline capture, offline transcription, local inference and remote tools are different capabilities.

iOS `Voice/ConversationCoordinator.swift` currently coordinates separate Theora realtime and FERAL histories. `FeralConnectionManager.swift`, `FeralGlassesRelay.swift` and `Services/AmbientTranscriptSync.swift` provide partial bridging and a durable transcript outbox. Preserve real outbox/ACK behavior while unifying the record. Audit empty granted-capability fallback and shared photo-notification correlation; require per-request camera correlation, freshness, selected-device authority and revocation. Backend `/v1/node` and desktop `/v1/session` remain distinct contracts.

Test Theora health-temple glasses, Theora Eye camera/microphone glasses and Veepoo wristband independently. SDK symbols do not establish raw PPG/PTT or camera capability on another unit. Health records need units, time, device/firmware, quality and measured-versus-estimated labels; clinical claims require separate validation. Acceptance covers Bluetooth loss, sleeping/locked phone, system eviction versus force quit, permission denial, interruption, camera races and concurrent devices.

### Proactivity and social use

Schedulers, automations, plugins and direct skill calls must share interactive review/grant/spend/generation policy. Separate suggestion, draft, reviewed action and narrowly delegated autonomy. Enforce freshness, quiet hours, dismissal and rate/cost budgets. Pause must state whether it prevents new work or drains active work.

The October 1 implementation routes calming/breathing scene dispatch through
ToolRunner using the verified primary session. Missing ownership, pending review,
denial and uncertain results do not count as execution; loose chat autonomy does
not grant unattended confirmation-class scenes. Real-runner policy tests passed.
The central resolver also now preserves explicit denial when approval/read-only
metadata conflict. These corrections do not establish authorization for every
other proactive or external entry point. Test all entry paths, not only chat.

Social sharing is off by default. A review names recipients, exact records/fields, purpose and expiry. Missing/invalid scopes remain private, consistent with `security/sync_scopes.py` and peer grants. Revocation stops future exchange but cannot retract received copies. No automatic stranger identification, ambient-conversation sharing, health training or cross-account memory disclosure. Test two owner partitions and an adversarial peer.

### Payments and purchases

`skills/impl/web_actions.py` currently produces a preview (`purchased=false`, checkout unavailable), not a live transaction. Align manifest/UI language. `security/commerce.py` supplies spend/currency/merchant/audit checks, not merchant access or completed checkout.

Bind an expiring immutable quote to seller, product/variant/quantity, availability, currency, item cost/tax/shipping/total, address, funding-token reference, recurring terms and refund policy. Fresh wearer/user approval binds the exact quote; model intent is not payment authorization. Keep raw payment credentials out of prompts/logs.

Add atomic budget reservation, merchant idempotency, durable attempt/receipt ledger and timeout reconciliation. Never retry a possible charge blindly. Distinguish authorized, captured, fulfilled, refunded and disputed; report completion from merchant/payment evidence. Sandbox changed totals, expired quotes, duplicate/late callbacks, cancellation races and refunds before named live merchant acceptance. Browsing, checkout handoff and completed purchase are separate release stages.

### iMessage and external messaging

`skills/desktop_control/facade.py` exposes `desktop.messages.send` via Messages.app AppleScript and macOS Automation permission. It selects an iMessage service; the broader iMessage/SMS catalogue label is not proof of SMS or delivery. Script completion is not recipient receipt. No external message was sent during this verification.

Review binds sending owner/account, resolved recipient, exact text/attachments, source disclosure and expiry. Show all recipients; changed text requires fresh authority. Distinguish draft/composer handoff, dispatch and delivery evidence. Unknown sends must not auto-retry. Test recipient ambiguity, account/contact changes, denied Automation, escaping, duplicate confirmation and timeout with injectable adapters, then authorized test accounts. Verify supported public iOS routes separately; do not promise unrestricted background iMessage sending or Linux parity.

### Credentials, coding and oversight

Native deferred vault boot stays passive. Optional vault absence must not block credential-free local use; encrypted profiles remain limited with Security reachable. Existing unlock and `/api/security/agent-bootstrap` continuation are separate reviews. OS/startup work may continue after timeout; no automatic retry. Fresh initialization remains gated/unwired until genuine signed OS acceptance. Never reset/reseed personal Keychain as recovery.

Revocation requires immediate ingress fencing, generation invalidation, cancellation/drain of registered turns and owned producers before closing restored memory. Failed drain requires truthful restart/unknown state, not clean rollback. Fixtures cover mechanisms; real encrypted-profile lifecycle remains open.

OpenCode is reused execution infrastructure, not product authority. Reviews require concrete arguments/paths/working directory/effects; denial prevents dispatch and receipts identify actual outcomes. CLI `cli/main.py` and `api/routes/approvals.py` need owner/session-scoped decision receipts; sending a permission event does not prove execution. `api/routes/supervisor.py` pause does not prove all jobs stopped. Unify the action inbox across core, coding, hardware, delegation and payments while preserving action-specific reviews.

## Product fit, rollout and decisions

Validate recurring commitment recall and follow-through. Measure capture reliability, source accuracy, completed commitments, review comprehension, false interruptions, weekly retained use, willingness to pay and cost per verified useful outcome. Compare glasses and phone-only paths. Set thresholds/stopping rules before the cohort; repository review cannot establish product-market fit or uniqueness.

Roll out through isolated acceptance, signed owned-device acceptance, a supported consenting cohort and broader supported release. Flags fail closed and disclose unavailable features. Expand payments, social exchange and physiological coaching only after their own evidence and demand justify them.

Decisions: countries/health claims; Mac CPU/minimum OS; Linux strategy; iOS availability; hardware/firmware/vendor rights; local/hosted owner/operator; retention/deletion/backups; name/avatar/voice rights; providers/network disclosures; first messaging integration/merchant; payment access; price/support. Record owner/date/rationale and changed acceptance requirements. Open source also needs dependency/asset rights, secret scanning, installation instructions, threat model, vulnerability contact and maintenance policy. Publishing source is not production certification.
